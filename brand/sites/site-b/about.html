Our Story

We Started From Frustration.

In 2019, our founder watched a 20-year-old family restaurant lose 40% of its bookings in six months — not because the food got worse, but because three fake negative reviews spiked their Google average and nobody knew how to fight back.

That restaurant is still a client. They're now at 4.7 stars on Google with 380 reviews and a waitlist most weekends.

OptiRate was built on that single principle: the businesses that deserve to win should win. And in today's market, winning starts with your online reputation.

We're a team of 18 marketers, engineers, and reputation specialists across three continents. We've served 500+ businesses in 13 markets — plumbers to law firms, auto dealers to dental practices — and we've yet to find a reputation we couldn't improve.

We don't do vanity metrics. We don't do long-term contracts. We don't hide behind dashboards and reports. You'll know exactly what we're doing, why we're doing it, and what it's producing — every single week.

FAQ

Straight Answers.

Do you guarantee results?

We guarantee activity, transparency, and a money-back policy if we miss agreed milestones. We can't guarantee specific star ratings — platforms control that — but our track record speaks: 92% of clients see measurable improvement in 30 days.

How long before I see results?

Review generation campaigns typically show volume impact in 30–60 days. Reputation improvement varies by starting point — expect visible rating movement in 60–90 days for businesses starting below 3.5 stars.

Are your methods platform-compliant?

Yes. We never generate fake reviews or use review gating schemes. All review generation is opt-in outreach to your real customers. All reputation responses follow platform guidelines. We're 100% white-hat.

What industries do you specialise in?

Home services, healthcare, hospitality, automotive, legal, and professional services. These verticals represent 80% of our client base and we have proven playbooks for each.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

Never. All engagements are month-to-month. We believe in earning your business every single month, not locking you in and hoping for the best.

How does billing work?

Monthly retainer invoiced in advance. Cancel with 30 days notice. No setup fees, no hidden costs. The price we quote is the price you pay — no scope creep surprises.
