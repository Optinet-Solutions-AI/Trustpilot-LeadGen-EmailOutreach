We believe every business deserves its reputation.

Not the one the internet accidentally assigns it — the one it truly earns.

RateUp Global was founded on a simple observation: the businesses with the best online reputations aren't always the best businesses. And the best businesses don't always have the reputation they deserve. That gap is a problem we can fix.

We started in 2018, working with a handful of restaurant owners who were watching competitors with weaker food and worse service outrank them on Google simply because those competitors were better at asking for reviews. The injustice was infuriating — and the solution was learnable.

Today we serve over 1,200 clients across 18 countries, from independent tradespeople to multi-location healthcare providers. Every engagement starts with the same belief: your reputation should be a genuine reflection of the work you put in. Our job is to make sure it is, and then to amplify it.

We are not a set-and-forget software tool. We are a team of strategists, writers, SEO specialists, and outreach experts who work inside your business as an extension of your team. We learn your voice, your customers, and your market — and we build a reputation strategy that compounds over time.
