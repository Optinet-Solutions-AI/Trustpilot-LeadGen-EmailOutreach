Our Story

Marketing built for the heart of the community

We started OptiRate because we believed local businesses deserved the same quality marketing as national chains — but with a team that actually knows and cares about their community.

Who We Are

A team that roots for local first

We founded OptiRate after watching talented, hard-working local business owners get burned by agencies that over-promised and under-delivered. Generic tactics built for national brands don't work when your customer lives three streets away and chose you on a neighbour's recommendation.

We built a different kind of agency — one that starts by listening, learns the suburb and the sector, and builds strategies that feel native to the community they serve. We're small enough to care deeply about every single client and experienced enough to deliver real, measurable results.

Twelve years on, we've helped more than 500 local businesses across cafés, trades, health practices, retail, and professional services. Our proudest moments aren't slides in a pitch deck — they're the business owners who call us on their anniversary to say their venue is finally full.

Our Values

The four things we never compromise on

Values aren't a wall poster. They're the questions we ask ourselves before every decision.

Honesty over hype

We don't promise rankings in 48 hours or guaranteed ROI before we've done the groundwork. We tell you what's achievable, when, and why — and then we deliver it.

Community first

Every market is different. We research your neighbourhood, understand your customer culture, and build campaigns that feel like they belong there — not imported from a national playbook.

Transparency in everything

You own your accounts, your data, and your results. We report every number — the good ones and the ones we're fixing — because you deserve to know exactly what you're paying for.

Long-term thinking

Quick wins are nice, but durable growth is the goal. We build the foundations — reviews, rankings, relationships — that keep working for you long after the campaign ends.
