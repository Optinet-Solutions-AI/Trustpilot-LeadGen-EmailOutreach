Our Story

Built for businesses that refuse to be average.

OptiRate was founded on a single conviction: that small and mid-size businesses deserve the same quality of marketing intelligence that large enterprises take for granted — without the agency bloat, the hidden fees, or the twelve-week onboarding.

We are a lean team of specialists in reputation management, search, paid media, and growth engineering. We work with a focused roster of clients so we can give each one the attention their business deserves.

Our Values

What we stand for

The principles that guide every decision, every campaign, every conversation.

P

Precision

We don't spray and pray. Every campaign is built on data, designed for a specific audience, and measured against clear outcomes.

T

Transparency

You see everything — the numbers, the decisions, the rationale. No black boxes. No inflated vanity metrics. Just honest reporting.

A

Accountability

We stand behind our results. If a campaign underperforms, we investigate, iterate, and own the outcome — no excuses, no extra invoices.

L

Long-Term Thinking

Quick wins are nice; compounding results are transformative. We design strategies that build durable competitive advantages, not sugar-rush spikes.

R

Relationships

We work with a focused client roster deliberately. Fewer clients means deeper knowledge of your market, faster responses, and better outcomes.

I

Innovation

The digital landscape shifts constantly. We invest in research, tooling, and methodology so our clients benefit from what's working right now — not two years ago.
